\subsection{The lead after fine-tuning}
\label{sec:ft-result}

\paragraph{Hypothesis and test.}
E6 part (b) asked whether OGC's Gram-metric VQ still beats C3DGS's own colour VQ at equal rate after C3DGS's 5,000-iteration fine-tuning, on the seven gate scenes (P\_FT, BD-PSNR of \code{ogc\_gram\_ft} against \code{c3dgs\_ft}, computed as P1 with the fine-tuned rows). % Am19 c; s21
It used E5's three criteria on fresh processes, four per scene, each fine-tuning both rows, so that the fine-tuned rows have a curve over the three colour thresholds. % Am19 c; s21
The hypothesis was formed after seeing E5's results on these scenes, so this is a pre-registered test on them and not a held-out claim. % Am19 c; s21; CLAIMS group F limits

\paragraph{Result (pre-registered).}
E6 part (b) passed as pre-registered: after fine-tuning OGC's VQ led C3DGS's own at equal rate with $\bar P_{\mathrm{FT}} = 0.0425$ dB, positive on 6 of 7 scenes, and $\bar P_{\mathrm{FT}} = 4.9\,\mathrm{SE}_{\mathrm{noise}}$ against the required 2 ($2\,\mathrm{SE}_{\mathrm{noise}} = 0.0174$ dB). % s21 "The verdict"; F1
The one negative scene was drjohnson, at $-0.0046$ dB (Table~\ref{tab:t2a}). % s21 per-scene table; F1
No scene was dropped and no primary row or value was missing. % s21 "The verdict"; F1

\paragraph{How firm the result is (descriptive, not pre-registered).}
Leaving out any one scene (Table~\ref{tab:t2b}), every case still met all three criteria, with $\bar P / \mathrm{SE}_{\mathrm{noise}}$ from 3.88 to 5.46, but 6 of the 7 cases met the count with exactly 5 positive scenes, so the count is the tight criterion. % s22 a; F5
Across scenes P\_FT had a standard deviation of 0.0373 dB ($t = 3.0$), which $\mathrm{SE}_{\mathrm{noise}}$ does not measure. % s22 a; F5
Under the standard error that matches one curve per scene, $\mathrm{SD}_{\mathrm{pool}}/\sqrt{n}$, $\bar P_{\mathrm{FT}}$ lay 3.45 standard errors above zero against the registered 4.9, and the leave-one-out cases 2.75 to 3.86; criterion 3 still held in every case, and the registered verdict stands under its pre-registered rule. % s25; F1, F5
With OGC's bytes set to C3DGS's at each $j$, a counterfactual and not equal rate, the mean was $+0.0458$ dB against $\bar P_{\mathrm{FT}} = +0.0425$ dB, so the result is not produced by where the bytes fall. % s22 c; F7

\paragraph{Against the lead before fine-tuning (descriptive).}
As a ratio of means P\_FT was 0.1195 of P1 replicated, with per-scene ratios from $-0.017$ to $+0.331$; the two measures average over different byte ranges, so the ratio is not the share of the earlier lead that survives at any one rate. % s22 b; F6
E5's own $j = 0$ comparison after fine-tuning, $+0.0604$ dB at unequal bytes, measures a different quantity on separate runs; neither value replaces the other. % s19 secondaries; A5; CLAIMS conflict 3

\paragraph{Where along the curve (descriptive, not pre-registered).}
On all seven gate scenes the fine-tuned rows' PSNR difference at the same $j$ rose from $j = -1$ to $j = +1$: from $-0.119$ to $+0.023$ dB at $j = -1$, $+0.024$ to $+0.112$ dB at $j = 0$, and $+0.061$ to $+0.379$ dB at $j = +1$. % s22 d; F8
These points are not rate-matched: at $j = +1$ OGC's row had 3.5\% to 7.9\% more bytes, and at $j = -1$ 0.3\% to 7.9\% fewer. % s22 d; s21 secondary 2; F8
After fine-tuning OGC's \code{.npz} was smaller than C3DGS's at $j = -1$ on every scene and larger at $j = +1$ on every scene, while before fine-tuning it was larger at every point. % s21 secondary 2; F3
The rising pattern is scoped to the gate scenes: on the development scenes it rose on flowers but fell on train and was not monotone on treehill. % s23 e; F8, G5

\subsection{Why the lead shrinks (E7): design}
\label{sec:ft-why}

\paragraph{Question.}
Before fine-tuning OGC's VQ leads C3DGS's own at equal rate by $\bar P = 0.3556$ dB (P1 replicated), and after it by $\bar P_{\mathrm{FT}} = 0.0425$ dB (Section~\ref{sec:ft-result}). % s21; F1, F2
E7, registered after E6's verdict and before any E7 data, asks which of the factors that fine-tuning moves carries the shrink. % Am20 a; Am20 b
Its hypotheses were formed after E5's and E6's results on these seven scenes, so it tests interventions within each scene and makes no held-out claim. % Am20 b

\paragraph{Arms.}
Every E7 process forks C3DGS at the colour VQ, as E5 and E6 do, and fine-tunes each method's table from the same copy of the model, its tables, its quantizer state and its random states, so that the arms differ only in what fine-tuning may move. % Am20 a; Am20 d; Am17 b
Arm R is C3DGS's own fine-tuning (5,000 iterations, both colour grids re-fitting through their observers), as in E6. % Am20 d
Arm G freezes both colour grids at their pre-fine-tuning state and trains everything else C3DGS trains; arm CG also freezes the $K$ codewords through a gradient hook; arm K trains only the codewords; arm O freezes each method's own grid, fitted to its own table. % Am20 d
Process \code{p0} repeats arm R from the same state (the replicate), so that the pair differs only by the GPU's nondeterminism. % Am20 d
Two unfine-tuned rows, \code{c3dgs\_own} and \code{ogc\_gram\_own}, save the same tables with each method's own grid, so that P1 can be read without OGC's codewords clamped by the shared grid; the own grid also changes the step, so these rows measure clamping and step together. % Am20 d; Am20 m
An inverted guard fails any fine-tuned row whose frozen tensors, row blocks or grids move during fine-tuning. % Am20 e

\paragraph{Primaries and tests.}
For an arm X, $\mathrm{BD}_X$ is the BD-PSNR of OGC's fine-tuned row of that arm against C3DGS's, computed as P\_FT over the seed-0 curve at $j = -1, 0, +1$. % Am20 f
The two primaries are, per scene, $\Delta_{\mathrm{refit}} = \mathrm{BD}_G - \mathrm{BD}_R$, what freezing the colour grids does to OGC's lead, and $\Delta_{\mathrm{cb}} = \mathrm{BD}_{CG} - \mathrm{BD}_G$, what freezing the codewords does to it under the fixed grid. % Am20 f
Their noise is E5's, from the $j = 0$ seed pair of each primary's paired difference, and their standard error is $\mathrm{SE} = \mathrm{SD}_{\mathrm{pool}}/\sqrt{n}$, the one that matches one curve per scene. % Am20 b; Am20 f; s25
Each primary is tested for superiority (two-sided, in E5's criteria form, with at least $\lfloor (7n + 9)/10 \rfloor$ scenes of the mean's sign) and for equivalence by two one-sided tests at a margin of $\delta = 0.07$~dB.\TODO{verify} % Am20 f
Each test type is one family of the two primaries, Holm-corrected at a family-wise 0.05: the primary with the greater statistic is tested first, and the second only if the first succeeds.\TODO{verify} % Am20 f; Am20 note 2
At $n = 7$ the critical values are 2.841 and 2.365 for superiority and 2.365 and 1.895 for equivalence.\TODO{verify} % Am20 f
The margin is 22\% of the gap the shrink closed, the smallest round value with about 80\% power in Holm's first position under both the pessimistic and the scene-spread noise assumptions taken from E6.\TODO{verify} % Am20 f
A robustness reading recomputes both tests with a BD-level standard error from arm R's seed-0 and seed-1 curves, $\mathrm{SE}_{\mathrm{BD}} = \sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{SD}_{\mathrm{BD}}/\sqrt{n}$; a primary whose outcome differs under it is reported as fragile, and the outcome under SE remains the one of record. % Am20 f
A primary is incomplete with fewer than five scenes after drops, or with one of its rows missing after one rerun of its process. % Am20 f
The registered outcomes are superior (of either sign), equivalent, both or neither, each with a reading written before any data. % Am20 h
Secondary measures, reported without bars, read codebook training alone (K against CG and R), the own grid in fine-tuning (O against G), P1 with each method's own grid, P1 and P\_FT replicated, BD-level noise from a seed-1 curve, the replicate pair, the guards' logs and the bytes. % Am20 g

\paragraph{Sessions.}
Each of the seven scenes is split into a seed-0 half, the three seed-0 processes, so that every arm's curve comes from one session and one Kaggle image, and a seed-1 half; the halves run in eleven Kaggle sessions on two T4 GPUs, seven seed-0 sessions with one scene each and then four seed-1 sessions. % Am20 j; Am20 note 1
The split rests on an estimate from E5's time model, not on a measurement, and each later session restores the earlier sessions' result files from their committed bundles, checked at ingest. % Am20 note 1; Am20 note 3; Am20 note 4; Am20 note 5

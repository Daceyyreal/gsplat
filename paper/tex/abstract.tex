OGC's colour vector quantiser (VQ) weights a Gaussian's spherical-harmonic colour by what the training cameras saw, and OGC's paper reports a large gain inside the C3DGS compression pipeline before C3DGS's fine-tuning and a small one after it. % s19 "Secondaries"; A4, A5
This paper tests that in three pre-registered experiments inside C3DGS, on INRIA's published checkpoints of seven gate scenes, running OGC's released VQ on this project's estimate of OGC's metric. % s19; A1, A12
Before fine-tuning, OGC's VQ beat C3DGS's own at equal rate on all seven scenes (mean BD-PSNR 0.3558 dB), and the Gram metric beat its trace in OGC's own Lloyd (0.1874 dB). % s19 "The verdict"; A2, B1
After fine-tuning the lead was small but survived: 0.0425 dB at equal rate, positive on 6 of 7 scenes. % s21 "The verdict"; F1
A third experiment then held factors fixed within each process: neither the colour grids' re-fit nor codebook training explained the shrink beyond $\pm 0.07$~dB in the mean, each being equivalent to zero and not superior. % s26 "The verdict"; H1, H2
Report-only and post-hoc readings of the other arms point to fine-tuning the parameters outside the grids and the codebook (the kept splats' colours, opacity, scaling and rotation), which the experiment does not separate. % s26 "What section 26 settles"; H5
The second and third experiments reuse the gate scenes, so neither is a held-out test. % s21; s26; F1, H1

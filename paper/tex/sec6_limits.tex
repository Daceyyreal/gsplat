\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limits}

\paragraph{An estimated metric.}
The replication ran OGC's released \code{gram\_kmeans}, unedited, on this project's 16-probe estimate of OGC's observation Gram, which equals it in expectation, and not on OGC's own Gram computation, so P1 and P2 replicate OGC's VQ given an estimated metric. % Am17 b; A12
On train the estimate's total trace was 0.919 of the exact Gram's, for a reason the files do not settle, and no gate scene has a comparison with the exact Gram. % s16; A12, E4

\paragraph{One host and one set of checkpoints.}
Every comparison ran inside one host, C3DGS at commit \code{2a234af5} with its recorded build and run deviations, on INRIA's published checkpoints, on T4 GPUs with one software image per experiment. % s19; A1, E1; CLAIMS common E5 limits
Whether OGC's lead carries over to other hosts, to checkpoints trained another way or to other GPUs is not tested. % CLAIMS common E5 limits
The pre-registration's statement that no pixel, splat value, render or metric of the seven scenes had been read before the replication's code holds for INRIA's checkpoints of those scenes only: earlier experiments of this project had read five of them with its own gsplat checkpoints, as disclosed in advance. % Am17 erratum; Am15 d; CLAIMS common E5 limits
The comparison with OGC's paper is not like for like: its host results are nine Mip-NeRF 360 scenes in its own evaluation with one run each, against seven scenes here, four of them shared, under protocol~ii. % s19; A4; CLAIMS Gap 4
OGC's paper does not say which C3DGS commit or which VQ ridge produced its tables, or how much they vary from run to run. % RELATED_WORK_OGC.md 2 g; CLAIMS Gap 12

\paragraph{Seven scenes and three points per curve.}
The gate is seven scenes: four from Mip-NeRF 360, one from Tanks and Temples and two from Deep Blending. % Am15 d; CLAIMS common E5 limits; A1
The fine-tuning test reuses them, and its hypothesis was formed after the replication's results on them, so it is not a held-out claim. % Am19 c; CLAIMS group F limits; F1
Each BD value is a parabola through three points, one seed-0 process per point, so each P rests on six measurements, and the seed noise is measured at $j = 0$ only, not at the level of the BD value. % s19; A2; CLAIMS Gap 8
$\mathrm{SE}_{\mathrm{noise}}$ is seed noise, not scene spread, and its registered $\sqrt{2n}$ divisor is that of a two-seed mean; with $\mathrm{SD}_{\mathrm{pool}}/\sqrt{n}$ the criterion-3 margins of P1, P2 and P\_FT are 77.7, 790 and 3.45 standard errors, and every criterion still holds. % s25; A2, F1
With $n = 7$ the count criterion is the tight one: in six of the seven leave-one-out cases P\_FT met it with exactly five scenes. % s22 a; F5

\paragraph{The AC grid's re-fit: bytes only.}
Fine-tuning re-fits C3DGS's int8 AC colour grid, widening it for OGC's codebook, and the records show what that does to the bytes; its effect on the PSNR lead is untested. % s24; E13, F9
The exploratory correlations across scenes and processes run against the re-fit as the cause of the shrinking lead, and none of them is a test. % s24 item 6; E18
Before fine-tuning OGC's AC codebook was clamped at the save in every process, so P1 itself was measured with a clamped codebook, and the clamping's effect on PSNR is also untested. % s24 item 2; E17

\paragraph{The development scenes are descriptive.}
E6 part (a), on flowers, treehill and train, has no criterion: its three predictions were checked, not judged, and none of its values is pooled with the gate scenes' results. % Am19 b; s23; G1
Its third prediction held on treehill and train because P\_FT was negative there. % s23 "The predictions"; G1, G2
Train's P\_FT is not interpreted: its fine-tuned rows moved by up to 0.19 dB between runs, so fine-tuning noise dominates it. % s23 a; G3

\paragraph{Evaluation and reproducibility.}
Protocol~ii and C3DGS's own evaluation differ by a scene-dependent amount, up to 0.429 dB on kitchen, for reasons the files do not show; the lead before fine-tuning is about the same under both. % s20; E14, A9
Seeded C3DGS runs are not reproduced on the GPU, so every experiment's numbers come from its own processes and no row is reused across experiments. % s15; E2; CLAIMS group F limits

\paragraph{Why the lead shrinks.}
Why OGC's lead shrinks with fine-tuning is the subject of a further pre-registered experiment (E7), whose results are not reported in this version. % Am20 a; CLAIMS Gap 3
